Οὖτις (Outis) is Ancient Greek for “nobody”. In Book 9 of the Odyssey, Odysseus gives the name to the Cyclops Polyphemus, and when the giant is blinded and cries out that Nobody has hurt him, no one comes to help. In Turkish: hiç kimse.
