Privacy

This page explains what is measured when you visit this site, and how to switch it off.

Who runs this site

The site owner, who writes as “hiç kimse” (nobody in particular). To ask a question or exercise a data protection right, write to adaptivesubject@gmail.com.

What is measured

The site uses Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC), to learn how many people read which pages. This can include page views and clicks on a few buttons, approximate location (country and city), browser, device type, screen size and language. Analytics sets cookies (_ga and _ga_*) holding a random identifier. Advertising features are not used.

Legal basis and retention

The legitimate interest of the site owner in understanding how the site is used. Analytics data is meant to be kept for no longer than 14 months. Google may process it on servers outside your country.

Turning it off

Measurement is on by default and no consent banner is shown. You can switch it off at any time from Privacy settings in the footer of every page. When it is off, the analytics cookies are deleted and no further measurements are sent. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, measurement stays off unless you choose otherwise.

What stays in your browser

Your choices are stored in your browser’s local storage and are never sent to a server: the privacy choice (hk.consent), the theme (hk.theme) and the Tree or Table view (hk.view).

Hosting and external links

The hosting provider may keep technical server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. Fonts, scripts and images are served from this site; nothing else is loaded from third parties apart from Google Tag Manager and Analytics described above.
