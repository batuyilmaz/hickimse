Nostratic v2.0Pronouns

I, you, we, you all, who

Personal and interrogative pronouns across the same eight language families. Pronouns change slowly and are rarely borrowed, which makes them some of the most quoted evidence for Nostratic. Forms are transcribed in IPA; every Nostratic root on this page is tentative.
