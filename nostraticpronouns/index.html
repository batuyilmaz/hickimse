Nostratic v2.0Pronouns

I, you, he, we, you all, they, who, what

Personal and interrogative pronouns across the same eight language families. Pronouns change slowly and are rarely borrowed, which makes them some of the most quoted evidence for Nostratic. Forms are transcribed in IPA; an asterisk marks a reconstructed form. Every Nostratic root and sound path on this page is hypothetical, marked with a question mark, and open to revision as the comparison develops.
